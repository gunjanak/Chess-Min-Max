Rules: On your turn, pick one heap and remove any number of sticks from it (at least 1). In Normal mode the player who takes the last stick wins. In Misère mode the player who takes the last stick loses.

The Math: A position is a losing position (P-position) if and only if the XOR (nim-sum) of all heap sizes equals 0. The AI always plays to leave a nim-sum of 0. When nim-sum ≠ 0 the AI wins with perfect play; when nim-sum = 0 you have the advantage!
