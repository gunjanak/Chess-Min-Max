Rules: On your turn, pick any of your pits (south row) with seeds. Seeds are sown counter-clockwise one by one into each pit and your store — but skip the opponent's store. Extra turn: If your last seed lands in your store, you get another turn. Capture: If your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture those seeds plus all seeds in the opposite pit. Game end: When all pits on one side are empty. Remaining seeds go to that player's store. Most seeds wins.
